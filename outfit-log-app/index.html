An outfit log app with no account

Rotation is a closet and outfit log. Log what you wear, see what you actually wear, and keep everything on your own device.

No accountFree in betaStays on your device

1What you log

  • The pieces you wore, picked from your closet.
  • A photo of the fit.
  • The occasion and time of day.
  • A rating for the look.

You can log up to two outfits a day.

2Plan ahead on the calendar

The calendar shows outfit photos on each day, so you can see what you wore and plan what comes next.

3See what you actually wear

Rotation shows what you wear most, which pieces and shoes have been resting, and what each piece costs per wear. Here is how cost per wear works.

4A wardrobe app with no account

There is no account and no sign-up. Your closet, outfits and photos are stored on your device. In Settings you can save a backup to iCloud or Files and load it on another device.

Rotation runs in your phone's browser today. Add it to your Home Screen and it opens like any other app. App Store and Google Play versions are coming soon.

5Share what you wear

Turn an outfit, a single piece or your monthly recap into an image for Instagram or X. Prices and where you bought something are never shown.

Questions

What is an outfit log app?

An outfit log app is an app where you record what you wear each day. Rotation lets you pick the pieces from your closet, add a photo, tag the occasion and time of day, and rate the look.

Do I need an account to use Rotation?

No. There is no account and no sign-up. Your closet, outfits and photos are stored on your own device.

How many outfits can I log in a day?

You can log up to two outfits a day.

Is Rotation free?

Rotation is free during the beta. Pricing will be announced before it launches in the App Store and Google Play.
