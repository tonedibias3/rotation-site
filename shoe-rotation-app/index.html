A shoe rotation app

A shoe rotation is the set of pairs you actually wear. Rotation keeps it next to your clothes so you can see which shoes get worn and which ones just sit there.

Why track your shoes

It is easy to fall back on the same two pairs and forget the rest of the shelf. You cannot see that pattern until you write it down. Logging shoes each day makes it obvious.

What Rotation gives your shoes

  • A shoes section next to tops, bottoms, outerwear, accessories and scents.
  • Shoe of the day. Set it daily, whether or not you log a full outfit.
  • A view of which shoes have been resting.
  • A share card for just your shoes. Choose light or dark, square or story, for Instagram or X.

Prices and where you bought a pair are never shown on a share card.

Tips for rotating pairs

  • Many sneaker owners give a pair a day off between wears so it can dry out and keep its shape.
  • Plan the week on the calendar so the pair you want is not the one you wore yesterday.
  • Check what has been resting before you reach for your usual pair.

Your shoes, your device

There is no account and no sign-up. Your closet and photos stay on your device. In Settings you can save a backup to iCloud or Files and load it on a new phone.

Questions

What is a shoe rotation?

A shoe rotation is the set of pairs you wear in turn, instead of wearing the same pair every day.

Can I log my shoes in Rotation?

Yes. Rotation has a shoes section next to tops, bottoms, outerwear, accessories and scents, and you can set a shoe of the day.

Can I share just my shoes?

Yes. You can turn your shoe of the day into an image for Instagram or X, whether or not you log a full outfit.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-up. Your data stays on your device.
