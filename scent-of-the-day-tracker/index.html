Scent of the day tracker

If you own more than a couple of fragrances, it is easy to reach for the same bottle every time. Rotation lets you log your scent of the day and keep a fragrance rotation next to your clothes.

No accountFree in betaStays on your device

1Log the scent, not just the outfit

Set a scent of the day by itself, or log it with a full outfit. You do not need to log a whole fit to log what you smelled like.

2One place for your whole rotation

Scents have their own section in Rotation, right next to your tops, bottoms, shoes, outerwear and accessories. Your fragrance rotation lives in the same place as the rest of your closet.

3Share your scent

  • Turn your scent of the day into an image for Instagram, X or anywhere else.
  • Share just the scent, whether or not you log a full outfit.
  • Choose light or dark, square or story, and show names, brands, both or neither.

Prices and where you bought something are never shown on a share card.

4Add context

When you log an outfit you can tag the occasion and time of day and rate the look. Over time you build a record of what you wore and when.

5Private by default

No account and no sign-up. Everything stays on your device, and you can save a backup to iCloud or Files.

Questions

What is a scent of the day?

A scent of the day is the fragrance you wear on a given day. In Rotation you can set it on its own or log it with a full outfit.

Can I share just my scent?

Yes. You can turn your scent of the day into an image for Instagram or X, whether or not you log a full outfit.

Can I keep fragrances and clothes in one app?

Yes. Scents have their own section in Rotation, next to tops, bottoms, shoes, outerwear and accessories.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-up. Your data stays on your device.
